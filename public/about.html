About Whiskers & Haven

Dedicated to rescuing, nurturing, and finding loving forever homes for cats in need.

Our Mission

Whiskers & Haven is a non-profit feline adoption center founded in 2020. We believe every cat deserves a warm, loving home filled with cozy nap spots, gentle head scratches, and endless purrs. Our team works tirelessly to rescue, rehabilitate, and rehome cats of all ages, breeds, and backgrounds.

From senior cats needing a quiet retirement home to playful kittens ready for their first adventure, we match each feline with the perfect family. We provide full veterinary care, spaying/neutering, vaccinations, and behavioral support to ensure every cat is healthy and ready for adoption.

Our Values

Compassion

Every cat receives individualized care, love, and respect regardless of age, medical condition, or background.

Responsibility

We ensure all cats are spayed/neutered, vaccinated, and thoroughly health-checked before adoption.

Lifelong Bonds

We carefully match cats with adopters based on lifestyle, experience, and home environment for lasting happiness.

Community

We educate the community on responsible pet ownership and provide resources for cat care beyond adoption.
